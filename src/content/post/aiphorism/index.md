**AIphorism**  
/ˌeɪ.aɪˈfɔː.rɪ.zəm/  
*noun*

A short, polished-sounding statement generated by artificial intelligence, typically built from a familiar rhetorical template and offering the appearance of insight without much specificity, originality, or substance.

Common forms include:

- **"Not X, but Y."**  
  *Leadership is not about having all the answers, but about asking the right questions.*

- **"One X, one Y, both Z."**  
  *One team, one vision, both driven by purpose.*

- **"It's more than X—it's Y."**  
  *It's more than a product—it's a movement.*

- **"In a world where X, Y matters more than ever."**  
  *In a world where change is constant, adaptability matters more than ever.*

- **"At its core, X is about Y."**  
  *At its core, innovation is about solving human problems.*

- **"The future of X isn't Y. It's Z."**  
  *The future of work isn't remote. It's flexible.*

**Usage:**  
*The presentation was full of AIphorisms about innovation, resilience, and unlocking human potential.*

**Derived forms:**  
**AIphoristic**, *adjective* — resembling or containing AI-generated pseudo-wisdom.  
**AIphorize**, *verb* — to rewrite an ordinary statement as polished AI prose.

**Etymology:**  
A blend of **AI** and **aphorism**, with an implied association with AI-generated slop.

**Compare:**  
An **aphorism** compresses an observation into a memorable sentence.  
An **AIphorism** compresses a rhetorical template into something that sounds like an observation.
